# Privacy Policy for Physical Presence Calculator

**Last updated:** August 12, 2026

Physical Presence Calculator ("the App") is an independent utility tool designed to help users log travel records and calculate physical presence in Canada. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Physical Presence Calculator does NOT collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any of your personal information.**

All data entered into the App—including your Permanent Residency (PR) date, travel dates, ports of entry, transport modes, and residency exemptions—is stored strictly:
- **Locally on your device** inside the App's isolated sandbox.
- **In your personal Apple iCloud account** (if iCloud sync is enabled on your device) to allow synchronization across your own iOS devices.

We do not operate external databases or servers to collect or store your travel records.

---

## 2. Analytics and Tracking

The App:
- Does **NOT** use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase).
- Does **NOT** contain third-party advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks.
- Does **NOT** track your location, background location, or user activity across other apps or websites.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App relies solely on native Apple iOS frameworks and Apple iCloud services provided directly by Apple Inc. Any data synced via iCloud is encrypted and handled in accordance with [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
